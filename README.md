markdown# OpenImago

> **Everyone has a pattern. Now you can see it.**

Open source behavioral prediction platform. Map anyone's behavioral operating system across every domain of their life simultaneously. Personal relationships. Professional behavior. Political alignment. Legal patterns. Financial history.

Built with Claude AI. MIT Licensed. Community powered.

## The Origin

This framework was discovered in a single conversation analyzing why Trump's foreign policy behavior mirrors his relationship patterns with his ex-wives. The connection was immediate and undeniable.

The same operating system that drives how someone treats a spouse drives how they treat a country. How a CEO behaves in a divorce predicts how he behaves in a boardroom crisis. How a politician treats former allies predicts how he treats foreign nations.

The pattern doesn't change. The target does.

That insight became a six stage framework. The framework became this platform.

---

## The Six Stages

| Stage | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | PURSUIT | Charm · Promise · Courtship |
| 2 | SECURED | Deal Made · Compliance Achieved |
| 3 | DOMINANCE | Control Established · Loyalty Tests Begin |
| 4 | ESCALATION | Pressure Applied · Public Humiliation |
| 5 | THRESHOLD | Breaking Point Imminent |
| 6 | BROKE | Defection · Rupture · Gone |

---

## Five Default Domains

- **Personal Relationships** — Intimate and family behavioral patterns
- **Geopolitical** — Nation state and international relationship patterns  
- **Domestic Base** — Coalition and constituency behavioral patterns
- **Tech/Corporate** — Institutional and organizational patterns
- **Inner Circle** — Close associate and advisor patterns

---

## The Demo

The platform launches with a pre-loaded behavioral profile for Donald Trump — 32 entities across 5 domains showing the same OS pattern running simultaneously across personal relationships, geopolitical decisions, and domestic political behavior.

This is the proof of concept that validates the framework.

---

## Features

- **Behavioral OS Tracker** — Kanban board showing all relationships across six stages
- **Radar Chart Visualization** — Cross domain pattern mapped visually
- **Know Their Pattern** — Seven question quiz showing where you sit in someone's OS
- **Analyze Anyone** — Full behavioral observation flow for any subject
- **Stage Distribution Analytics** — Visual breakdown of pattern concentration
- **Trajectory Prediction** — AI powered prediction of next likely behaviors
- **Custom Domains** — Add domains you don't see for any subject
- **Community Contribution** — Corroborated community data points
